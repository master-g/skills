01 · System principles

Rules before recipes.

These principles decide what the system should feel like when a component example does not cover the exact situation.

01

Earn the emphasis

Reserve coral, large type, and dark surfaces for moments that truly change interpretation or action.

02

Let structure breathe

Use spacing and fine rules to organize content before adding containers, fills, or ornament.

03

Write the interface

Specific labels and useful context carry more trust than decorative polish or unexplained iconography.

Named by purpose.

04 · Usage guardrails

Consistency is judgment.

The system stays recognizable when teams preserve its hierarchy and restraint, even when a page needs a new composition.

Do

Use contrast to explain

  • Give every accent color a semantic job.
  • Use rules and spacing before adding a card.
  • Keep labels concrete and context-specific.
  • Test each composition at 390 px and 1280 px.
Avoid

Do not decorate uncertainty

  • Do not use coral across competing actions.
  • Do not put every section in a rounded box.
  • Do not use dark surfaces as visual filler.
  • Do not replace descriptive labels with icons alone.

Accessibility is foundational

Text and essential controls must retain AA contrast, focus must remain visible, meaning cannot depend on color alone, and motion must respect reduced-motion preferences.
